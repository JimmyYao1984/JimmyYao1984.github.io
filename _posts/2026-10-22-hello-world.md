My name is **Jimmy Yao**, and this blog is where I will document my life from **2026 to 2036**—ten years of learning, growing, and working toward becoming a tour guide for international visitors.

This is not a story written after everything has worked out. It is a record of the journey as it happens, one day and one small step at a time.

## From Learning English to Guiding International Visitors

My journey begins with learning English. My goal is to build the language skills and confidence I need to welcome visitors from around the world, help them explore, and share local stories with them.

Along the way, I will document:

- How I start learning English and develop my study habits.
- My progress in listening, speaking, reading, and writing.
- The mistakes I make and the lessons I learn.
- How I prepare to become a tour guide for international visitors.
- My experiences meeting people, communicating across cultures, and guiding travelers.

The small moments matter, too: a new word I finally remember, a conversation I find the courage to start, or a difficult day when I keep going.

## Ten Years of My Life, Honestly Recorded

From 2026 to 2036, this blog will be more than a learning journal. It will be a personal record of my everyday life, my decisions, my challenges, and the moments that shape who I become.

**Everything I share here will be 100% true to my own life and experiences.** No invented achievements. No made-up encounters. No pretending that progress is always easy.

Some posts will celebrate milestones. Others will describe setbacks, uncertainty, or ordinary days. They all belong in the story.

## This Is Where I Begin

I do not know exactly what the next ten years will bring. But I know what I want to work toward: learning English, connecting with people from different countries, and becoming a tour guide who helps international visitors feel welcome.

This blog is my place to keep an honest record of that journey.

**I’m Jimmy Yao. This is my story, from 2026 to 2036.**
